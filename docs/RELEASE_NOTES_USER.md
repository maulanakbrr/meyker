# 🎉 What's New in Meyker! (Version 0.3.0)

We’ve been hard at work making Meyker the best financial tracker for you! Our latest update (v0.3.0) is packed with features designed to save you time and help you hit your financial goals faster. 

Here is everything new in this release:

### 🔁 Set it and Forget it: Recurring Transactions & Subscriptions!
Tired of manually entering your rent or Netflix subscription every month? We hear you!
* **Automated Entries:** You can now set up recurring income and expenses. Just tell us if it's daily, weekly, monthly, or yearly, and we’ll automatically log it for you when the due date hits!
* **Subscription Management:** Easily view, pause, or edit all your active subscriptions from a single dashboard. 

### 🎯 Stay on Track: Category Spending Budgets
Want to limit how much you spend on coffee or online shopping?
* **Monthly Spending Caps:** You can now set monthly target budgets for any of your expense categories.
* **Visual Health Indicators:** We’ve added dynamic progress bars so you know exactly where you stand. They are color-coded to keep you safe: Green (Looking good!), Yellow (Getting close!), and Red (Whoops, over budget!).

### 💰 Dream Big: Savings Goals Tracker
Whether you're saving for an Emergency Fund or a New Laptop, we are here to help you get there!
* **Personal Goals:** Create custom savings goals with your target amount, a deadline, and even pick a fun color.
* **Quick Deposits:** Easily contribute funds directly toward your goals and watch your progress grow!

### 📄 Share Your Success: Beautiful PDF Statements
Need a formal record for your taxes, accountant, or just for your own filing cabinet?
* **Professional Exports:** You can now generate branded, professional PDF financial statements with just one click.
* **Clear Summaries:** The PDFs include a clean breakdown of your expenses by category, percentage shares, and a color-coded transaction history table.

### 🏦 Time Saver: Bank Statement Imports
Got a lot of transactions in your bank account that you want to move into Meyker?
* **Bulk Uploads:** You can now upload your CSV or Excel (`.xlsx`) bank statements directly into the app!
* **Smart Auto-Detect:** Our engine automatically recognizes formats from major Indonesian banks (BCA, Mandiri, BRI, CIMB Niaga) to make importing a breeze.
* **Intelligent Auto-Matching:** Meyker will try to automatically guess the right category based on the transaction description so you don't have to categorize everything manually!
